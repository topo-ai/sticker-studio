# 图生视频接力提示词

```text
Animate the attached character master into one short looping chat reaction.
Identity invariants: [visible traits].
Action: [one articulation and emotional arc].
Camera locked, fixed scale, no scene transitions or added character.
Start/end: [compatible rest pose]. Duration: [requested and supported].
Background: [exact selected RGB screen or tool-native alpha if actually supported].
Keep existing props and costume stable; no text morphing, zoom or global flashing.
```

记录真实可用模型、输入图、实际参数和原视频路径。若要用户在外部生成，交付输入图+提示词+格式要求，等视频回来再抽帧；未收到不能称完成。先预览区间，再决定时间采样，不把片头和尾部水印混进表情。
